Delete your GharBook data

GharBook for Android

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open GharBook and go to Settings.
  2. Tap your Google account under Backup and sync.
  3. Tap Delete account and cloud data, then Delete.

Your cloud entries, your account and any pending family invites are deleted at once. Family books you created are ended for everyone. You leave the family books you joined, and the entries you added there stay with that family.

Without the app

Email a.mohit1998@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my GharBook data". The account and its cloud data are deleted within 30 days and you get a reply when it is done.

What is kept

Nothing is kept in the cloud after deletion. Crash reports, which never contain your entries, expire on their own after 90 days. Entries on your phone stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
