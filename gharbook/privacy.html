Privacy policy

GharBook for Android. Last updated 23 October 2026.

GharBook keeps a record of what you spend and receive each day. Everything works on your phone without an account. Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud backup and family books.

Data on your phone

Your entries, tags, repeats and settings are stored on your phone. They stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. Backup files you save are written where you choose and are not sent anywhere by GharBook.

Data in the cloud, only after you sign in

  • Your Google account name and email address, to sign you in and so family members can invite you.
  • Your entries, tags, repeats and saved items, so they come back after you reinstall or change phones.
  • Family books: entries in a family book, and the name and email of each member, are visible to every member of that book.

Cloud data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). It is encrypted in transit. Access rules allow only you, and members of a family book you joined, to read it.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, a crash report is sent through Firebase Crashlytics. It contains the error, the phone model, the Android version and an installation identifier. It does not contain your entries or amounts.

What GharBook does not do

  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No reading of SMS, contacts, location or files you have not picked.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings, tap your account, then Delete account and cloud data. This removes your cloud data, leaves every family book (ending the ones you created) and closes your account at once. Entries on your phone stay until you uninstall the app or clear its data. If you cannot open the app, see Delete your data.

Children

GharBook is meant for adults managing a household and is not directed at children.
