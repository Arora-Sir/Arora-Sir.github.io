Gravity Bridge

Your AI coding agent, now in your pocket.

Python Local-First 13+ stars

Why It Exists

Google's Antigravity 2.0 is a local AI coding assistant that runs on your laptop and reads your files, edits code, and runs commands - but it only opens at localhost, unreachable from your phone. GravityBridge is a secure local proxy that puts the AI chat and a phone file explorer on one page in your phone's browser, so you can write code, check git status, and move files between devices without touching a cable.

🛋️

Couch Coding

Write code, run tests, and check git commits from your phone while sitting on the couch.

📸

Quick Mobile UI Debugging

Screenshot a bug on your phone, pull it to your laptop via ADB, and ask the agent to fix it.

📂

Wireless Phone Drive

Browse your phone's /sdcard storage and pull files to your PC wirelessly, one tap.

🔐

Lock Screen & Brute-Force Protection

PIN-gated entry with CAPTCHA and a temporary IP block after failed attempts.
