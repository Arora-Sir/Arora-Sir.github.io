CopyClip

Copy on one device. Paste on the other. No cloud in between.

Instant bidirectional clipboard sync between Windows and Android over Tailscale - no app installed on the phone. No cloud relay, no account to sign into, just a documented, directly benchmarked local pipeline with a measured security and trust model. This is the project I'm iterating on the most right now.
