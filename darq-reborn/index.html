DarQ Reborn

Force Dark, per app, without asking your whole phone to go dark.

Android Kotlin ★ 84 stars

Why It Exists

DarQ provides a per-app selectable Force Dark option for Android 10 and above, using a root or Shizuku service to apply the theme seamlessly - no accessibility service required. This is a modded fork of KieronQuinn's original DarQ (now archived) that I maintain actively under my own package name, including a recent Auto Dark mode that mirrors your phone's live Dark Theme setting.

🎯

Per-App Selection

Choose exactly which apps get Force Dark instead of an all-or-nothing system toggle.

🔓

Shizuku or Root

Works via ADB-based Shizuku on non-rooted devices, or directly with root access.

🧩

Xposed / LSPosed Module

Bypasses apps that block Force Dark in their own code, for the stubborn ones.

🌗

Auto Dark Schedule

Sunrise-to-sunset scheduling, or follow your system's live Dark Theme setting.
